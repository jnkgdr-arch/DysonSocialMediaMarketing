Competitive research objective

Evaluation of two competitor social media sites

How iRobot and Bissell use voice, tone, customer response, and content formats to drive engagement.

Competitor #1: iRobot

  • Frequency: ranges from 3+ posts per week to 1 per month.
  • Voice: passionate, respectful, casual, funny, sympathetic, and informative depending on platform.
  • Content: mini reels, appliance features, promotions, company accomplishments, feature-led acting, and newsfeed-style updates.
  • Successful post: October 4, 2022 post received high likes, engagement, moderate shares, and visible positive reactions.

Competitor #2: Bissell

  • Frequency: 1–2 posts per week.
  • Voice: consistent, upbeat, casual, and friendly.
  • Content: information, casual questions, mini how-to videos, product promotions, company news, and testimonials.
  • Successful post: Recent recall post drew positive engagement and high shares because customers felt seen and heard.

Social audience competitor comparison

Followers/subscribers provided

Key competitive takeaways

  • Platform leaders: Bissell leads TikTok and Pinterest; iRobot leads LinkedIn; Dyson leads X/Twitter.
  • TikTok position: Dyson and iRobot have similar audiences, while Bissell’s lead creates a clear growth benchmark.
  • LinkedIn opportunity: Dyson’s major gap can become a channel for B2B credibility, employer branding, and professional storytelling.
  • Content action: Strengthen TikTok with short-form demonstrations, creator content, and product/problem-focused stories.
  • Whitespace: Bissell’s Pinterest lead signals an opportunity in cleaning routines, home organization, and product-care inspiration.

S.W.O.T Chart

Dyson social media strategic position

Strengths

  • Promotes products by referencing technology goals.
  • Posts company mission videos that advocate for change.
  • Has @askDyson to answer inquiries and localize customer questions.

Weaknesses

  • Poor customer service response.
  • Slow posting frequency.
  • Lacks humanity in voice and tone.

Opportunities

  • Best training and response for social media inquiries.
  • Create informative how-to posts on common product issues.
  • Find a new voice and tone for better audience connection.
  • Create polls for better customer engagement.

Threats

  • More efficient vacuums.
  • Customers not purchasing due to continuous product malfunction after purchase.
  • Cheaper priced vacuum.
